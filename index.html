Cross River State, Nigeria

Cross River State is a beautiful state in southeastern Nigeria known for its rich culture, natural scenery, and tourist attractions. The state is home to Calabar, its capital city, and attractions such as the Cross River National Park and the famous Obudu Mountain Resort. Cross River is also known for its colorful festivals and diverse cultural heritage.
